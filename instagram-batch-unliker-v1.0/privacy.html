Privacy

Instagram Batch Unliker

Privacy policyLocal processing

This extension is designed to operate locally in your browser. It does not collect, sell, advertise against, or transmit your Instagram login credentials, liked-post history, post media, usernames, or browsing history to a developer-operated server.

What the extension accesses

When you use the extension, it accesses the Instagram Likes page needed to select and unlike posts at your direction. It also stores your extension settings and run state locally using Chrome storage so the interface can remember your preferences and report progress.

What is not collected

The extension does not request your Instagram password and does not intentionally collect authentication tokens, cookies, direct messages, contacts, or unrelated website data.

Sharing

No Instagram page data or extension settings are intentionally transmitted to third parties by the extension.

Limited Use

The extension uses information received from Chrome and Instagram only to provide its single purpose: helping you manage posts you have liked on Instagram. It does not use that information for personalized advertising, sale to data brokers, or unrelated profiling.

Changes

If the extension's data practices change, the privacy policy and Store disclosures will be updated before the changed practices are introduced.
